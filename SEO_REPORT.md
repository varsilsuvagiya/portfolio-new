## 9. Google Search Console — steps (external, not done)

1. Deploy, and set `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL` to the real domain (e.g. `https://yourdomain.com`).
2. https://search.google.com/search-console → **Add property** → *Domain* (DNS TXT record at your registrar — recommended) or *URL prefix* (HTML tag: copy the `content` value into env `GOOGLE_SITE_VERIFICATION`, redeploy).
3. **Sitemaps** → submit `sitemap.xml`.
4. **URL Inspection** → `/`, `/about`, `/projects/olysim` → *Request indexing*.
5. Monitor **Pages** (indexing) and **Performance** (queries such as "Varsil Suvagiya") over the following weeks.
